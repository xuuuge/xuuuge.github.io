How it works & accuracy

Distance = √(ΔX² + ΔY²) × metres per unit. The default scale of 100 reproduces the reference example: (104.73, 64.35) → (100.35, 59.50) gives 653.51 m / 222.08°. Check the scale against your game map.

Bearing runs clockwise from north: 0° N, 90° E, 180° S, 270° W. X increases east; Y increases north. Map screenshots must be north-up and uniformly scaled. Click accuracy limits precision; zoom your screenshot in the browser if needed.

This calculates horizontal distance and compass bearing, not firing elevation, shell trajectory or terrain height. It does not automatically read coordinates from the game. Independently recreated from this reference; not affiliated with WARDOGS.
